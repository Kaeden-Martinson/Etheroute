Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Etheroute ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our Service.

NO KYC POLICY: Etheroute does not collect personally identifiable information like names, email addresses, or other identifying data. We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide our cryptocurrency withdrawal service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Username (your chosen identifier)
  • Password (hashed and encrypted)

2.2 Transaction Information

To process withdrawals, we collect and store:

  • Cryptocurrency wallet addresses (provided by you)
  • Transaction amounts and dates
  • Transaction status and blockchain confirmations
  • Chain/network information (ETH, BTC, SOL, LTC)

2.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Usage logs and timestamps
  • WebSocket connection data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Processing and completing cryptocurrency withdrawals
  • Providing real-time transaction status updates
  • Preventing fraud and illegal activities
  • Improving and maintaining our Service
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Security and technical support

4. Data Security

4.1 Encryption

All sensitive information, including passwords and wallet addresses, are encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols.

4.2 Access Control

Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel and systems necessary to provide the Service. We implement multiple security layers including firewalls and access controls.

4.3 Security Limitations

While we implement comprehensive security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

5. Data Retention

We retain user data for as long as necessary to provide our Service and maintain transaction records. Transaction history may be retained for compliance and dispute resolution purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Blockchain Networks

When you withdraw cryptocurrency, your transaction data is recorded on the blockchain, which is public and immutable. This is beyond our control.

6.2 RPC Providers

We use blockchain RPC providers to verify transactions. These providers may collect IP addresses and usage data according to their privacy policies.

6.3 Other Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers for infrastructure and support. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Data Access

You can request access to your account information at any time through your account dashboard.

7.2 Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data. However, we may retain transaction records as required by law.

7.3 Opt-Out

You can disable WebSocket connections and real-time notifications in your account settings.

8. Cryptocurrency Addresses and Privacy

Cryptocurrency transactions are pseudonymous but not anonymous. Your wallet addresses and transaction amounts are visible on the blockchain, even though your username is not directly linked.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware of any such collection, we will delete the information immediately.

10. International Users

Our servers are located in the United States. By using our Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States, which may have different privacy protections than your home country.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws and regulations. We may disclose information when required by law, such as in response to subpoenas, court orders, or government requests.

13. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach involving your information, we will notify you as soon as possible via email or account notification.

14. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@cryptosaas.io

15. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to request information about the categories of personal information we collect and the purposes for which we use it.

COMMITMENT TO PRIVACY: We are committed to maintaining your privacy while providing the best possible cryptocurrency withdrawal service. Your data is valuable and we treat it with utmost care.
