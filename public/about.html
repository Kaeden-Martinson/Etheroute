Why we built it

Taking a crypto payment should be the easy part of running a business. In practice it means picking a chain and hoping your customer holds it, reconciling a fee you were quoted against the one that actually landed, and handing identity documents to an intermediary who is only passing the money along.

Etheroute is that without the intermediary’s paperwork. You get an address on every chain we support, a confirmation you can watch block by block, and a review screen whose total is the number that sends. The keys stay encrypted, the fee stays where it was quoted, and nobody asks who you are.

How it works in practice

Four commitments, each one you can check against the product rather than take on faith.

01

No account to verify

A username and a password opens a basic account. There is no document upload, because there is no review queue behind it — you can take a payment the same day.

02

67 chains, one integration

Ethereum and its L2s, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Bitcoin, Litecoin and Solana — and 54 more EVM networks, with USDC and USDT on every EVM chain, plus Bitcoin Cash, Dogecoin and XRP Ledger — behind one API and one set of webhooks. Build against GET /api/chains and adding a network costs you nothing.

03

Your keys, encrypted at rest

Wallet keys are encrypted with AES-256 before they are stored, and withdrawals go out to an address you control. We hold the infrastructure, not a claim on your balance.

04

One fee, quoted before you confirm

A flat 5% platform fee and the network’s own cost, broken down on the review screen. No spread hidden in the rate, and no figure that moves between the quote and the send.
