Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Etheroute

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

▼

Click on "Get Started" in the top navigation bar and select the "Sign Up" tab. Enter your desired username and a strong password (minimum 8 characters). Agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click "Create Account". That's it!

No KYC Required: We don't ask for personal information like email or real name. Just a username and password.

Do you require KYC (Know Your Customer) verification?

▼

No. Etheroute does not require any KYC verification. We only collect a username and password. No personal information, email, ID, or address is needed. This is part of our privacy-first approach.

What if I forget my password?

▼

Since we don't collect email addresses, password recovery works through security questions. Contact our support team at t.me/etheroute with your username and we can help you reset your password securely.

Is my password secure?

▼

Yes. Passwords are hashed and encrypted using industry-standard encryption protocols. We never store plain-text passwords, and your password is only known to you.

Withdrawals & Transactions

Which cryptocurrencies can I withdraw?

▼

We support withdrawals on four major blockchain networks:

  • Ethereum (ETH) - ERC-20 compatible
  • Bitcoin (BTC) - Bitcoin network
  • Solana (SOL) - Solana network
  • Litecoin (LTC) - Litecoin network

What are the withdrawal fees?

▼

We charge a 5% platform fee on all withdrawals. Additionally, blockchain network fees may apply depending on current network conditions. Both fees are clearly displayed before you confirm your withdrawal.

Example: If you withdraw $100, the 5% fee is $5, so you receive $95 to your wallet (plus any blockchain fees).

How long does a withdrawal take?

▼

Withdrawal times depend on the blockchain network:

  • Ethereum: 30 seconds to 2 minutes
  • Bitcoin: 10 to 30 minutes
  • Solana: 15 to 45 seconds
  • Litecoin: 2 to 5 minutes

These times depend on network congestion. We provide real-time status updates via our REST-API.

Can I cancel a withdrawal?

▼

Once a withdrawal is confirmed on the blockchain, it cannot be reversed. Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible by design. Always verify the receiving address before confirming a withdrawal.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

▼

The minimum withdrawal amount is $5 USD (and gas fees) per chain. This ensures your withdrawal fees remain reasonable relative to your withdrawal amount.

Are there withdrawal limits?

▼

No daily withdrawal limits exist. You can withdraw as much as you want, as many times as you want. Limits depend only on your available balance.

Security & Privacy

Is my data safe?

▼

Yes. We use bank-grade encryption for all data in transit and at rest. Your wallet addresses are encrypted, and your transactions are secured. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Do you store my private keys?

▼

No. We never store, access, or have any control over your private keys. You maintain complete control of your wallets. We only process withdrawals on your behalf.

What data do you collect?

▼

We collect:

  • Username (your identifier)
  • Wallet addresses (to process withdrawals)
  • Transaction history

We do NOT collect personal information like email, phone, name, or location.

How do I delete my account?

▼

You can request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team at t.me/etheroute with your username. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days, except for transaction records which may be retained for legal compliance.

Technical

Do you offer an API?

▼

Yes! We offer a REST API for programmatic access. Check our API documentation for integration details and endpoints.

How do I get real-time status updates?

▼

We use REST-API connections to push real-time updates. Your client automatically subscribes to transaction and payout updates. You'll see live status changes as your withdrawal progresses.

What happens if the blockchain network is congested?

▼

During high network congestion, transactions may take longer and network fees may be higher. We use smart fee estimation to optimize your transaction costs. You can see estimated fees before confirming your withdrawal.
